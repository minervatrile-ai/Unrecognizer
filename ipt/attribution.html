Research Foundations & Attribution

When we explore themes like Terror Management Theory (TMT), outrage mechanics, and identity defense, it’s important to give credit to the social psychology and media research that laid the groundwork. Here are the primary sources and foundational works behind these ideas:

Foundation 01

1. Terror Management Theory (TMT)

Key Authors Sheldon Solomon, Jeff Greenberg, & Tom Pyszczynski
Essential Read The Worm at the Core: On the Role of Death in Life (2015)
Foundational Work Ernest Becker, The Denial of Death (1973)

Core Concept

How existential threat and mortality awareness trigger psychological defense mechanisms, rigid worldview defense, and intense identity protection.

Foundation 02

2. Algorithmic Outrage & Feed Dynamics

Author Tobias Rose-Stockwell
Essential Read Outrage Machine: How Social Media Exploits Our Innate Drive for Moral Doom (2023)

Core Concept

How modern recommendation feeds exploit deep human threat responses to manufacture outrage and drive engagement.

Foundation 03

3. Political Identity & Polarization

Author Ezra Klein
Essential Read Why We're Polarized (2020)

Core Concept

Group identity stacking, defensive reasoning, and how moral boundary enforcement shapes public discourse.
