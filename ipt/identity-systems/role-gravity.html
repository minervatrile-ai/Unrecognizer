🎭 IPT FIELD BRIEFING

ROLE GRAVITY

(Why some identities pull harder than others)

⚠️ CORE STRIKE

You don’t just take on roles.
Some roles take on you.

🧠 THE MECHANISM

Not all identities weigh the same.

Some are light:

  • casual observer
  • passerby
  • neutral participant

Easy to enter.
Easy to leave.

Others?

They carry mass.

  • the victim
  • the protector
  • the truth-teller
  • the rebel
  • the insider

These don’t just describe you…

👉 they anchor you.

🌀 THE PULL

Once a high-mass role activates:

  • attention narrows
  • emotion intensifies
  • interpretation shifts

Everything starts bending toward the role.

Neutral events?
👉 now they’re relevant

Unrelated signals?
👉 now they’re connected

Opposition?
👉 now it’s personal

🧲 WHY IT FEELS REAL

Because the role reorganizes perception.

You’re not pretending.

You’re experiencing through a filter that feels complete.

👉 The role doesn’t feel like a role
👉 It feels like truth

🎬 THE LOCK-IN

Once inside:

  • leaving feels like betrayal
  • softening feels like weakness
  • questioning feels like collapse

So the system stabilizes itself:

👉 defend
👉 reinforce
👉 repeat

🧱 THE FIELD REALIZATION

Most conflicts aren’t:

“Who is right?”

They’re:

👉 “Which role is active… and how much mass does it have?”

🪞 THE SNAP MOMENT

The shift happens when you catch it mid-pull:

“Wait…
is this me choosing this role…”

“…or am I being pulled into it?”

That question alone:

🧠 reduces the gravity
🧠 widens perception
🧠 restores movement

⚠️ FINAL NOTE

You don’t need to destroy roles.

You just need to stop confusing:

🎭 temporary positioning
with
🧍 permanent identity

🎯 Some roles don’t describe you.
They capture you.
