DEPROGRAMMING PROTOCOL MAGAZINE

ISSUE #2 • ROLE ASSIGNMENT • Circulation untracked.

🎭 CORE IDEA

ROLE ASSIGNMENT

You are not just reacting.
You are being placed.

Every signal doesn’t just ask what you think.
It assigns you a part:

  • defender
  • attacker
  • believer
  • skeptic
  • insider
  • outsider

Not randomly.
Predictably.

Most people don’t notice the assignment.
They just start performing.

🧾 MISSION STATEMENT

This issue exists to expose how roles are assigned in real time… and how quickly identity forms around them.

It teaches you to notice when:

  • you’re being positioned in a conflict
  • your reaction is being narrowed
  • your options are being simplified
  • your identity is being framed through opposition

…and to step out of the role before it hardens.

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

A signal appears.

Not neutral.
Framed.

It carries:

  • tone
  • implication
  • emotional direction

Before you think, you feel.

That feeling points you somewhere:

“Agree with this.”
“Fight this.”
“Be this kind of person.”

Now the system waits.

Not for truth.
For participation.

Once you react, the role locks in:

You’re now the one who…

  • always pushes back
  • always supports
  • always corrects
  • always defends

The more you repeat it,
the less it feels like a role.

It feels like you.

🛑 INTERRUPT IT

Catch the assignment early.

Right when something hits and you feel pulled—
pause.

Yeah. You again.
Still not commenting.
Good. Stay there a second longer.

Ask:

  • What role is this trying to put me in?
  • What would I do if I accepted it?
  • What would I do if I didn’t?

Feel the difference.
That’s the gap.

Inside it:

  • the script loosens
  • the urgency fades
  • the role stops sticking

Now choose:

  • play it
  • bend it
  • ignore it
  • walk away

Just don’t auto-accept.

🏢 NON-CORPORATE NOTICE

This publication does not assign roles.
It only reveals them.

No membership.
No identity offered.
No position required.

If you feel categorized,
that wasn’t us.

NOTICE • REFUSE • CHOOSE
🧠 You were about to play your part.
