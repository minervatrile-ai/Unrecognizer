DEPROGRAMMING PROTOCOL MAGAZINE

ISSUE #3 • ROLE GRAVITY • Circulation untracked.

🧲 CORE IDEA

ROLE GRAVITY

You don’t just get assigned a role.
You get pulled into it.

And the longer you stay…
the heavier it gets.

🧾 MISSION STATEMENT

This issue exists to expose why roles become difficult to leave… even when you know they’re roles.

It teaches you to notice when:

  • your past reactions are locking in your future ones
  • your audience expects consistency from you
  • your identity is tied to staying the same
  • leaving would cost you status, belonging, or clarity

…and why staying starts to feel easier than changing.

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

Once you play a role,
it doesn’t disappear.

It accumulates.

  • your past comments
  • your tone
  • your patterns
  • your position

All of it forms a kind of weight.

People begin to expect you to be that version of yourself.

The system reinforces it.

You start to feel it too:

“I’m the one who…”

  • calls things out
  • defends this side
  • pushes back
  • explains

Now leaving isn’t neutral.

Leaving feels like:

  • inconsistency
  • betrayal
  • weakness
  • loss of identity

So you stay.

Not because it’s true…
but because it’s stable.

🛑 INTERRUPT IT

You don’t break gravity with force.
You break it with awareness + deviation.

Notice when you feel locked into being predictable.
That’s the pull.

Pause there.

Yeah. Still you.
Still not commenting.
Still watching.
Good.

Ask:

  • What would I say if I wasn’t maintaining a role?
  • What would I do if no one expected consistency from me?
  • What would change if I allowed myself to shift?

Feel the resistance.
That resistance is the gravity.

Now do something slightly different:

  • soften your stance
  • don’t respond how you usually would
  • don’t reinforce your pattern
  • let the role go quiet

You don’t need to announce it.

Just… don’t feed it.

🏢 NON-CORPORATE NOTICE

This publication does not stabilize identities.

Consistency is not required.
Positions are not permanent.
You are allowed to change without notice.

Any pressure to remain the same
was not issued here.

NOTICE • REFUSE • CHOOSE
🧠 You felt the pull.
