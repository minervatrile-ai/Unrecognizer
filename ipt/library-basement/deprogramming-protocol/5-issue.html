DEPROGRAMMING PROTOCOL MAGAZINE

ISSUE #5 • AUDIENCE CAPTURE • Circulation untracked.

👁️ CORE IDEA

AUDIENCE CAPTURE

You’re not just in a role.
You’re being watched in it.

And once you are…
it becomes harder to leave.

🧾 MISSION STATEMENT

This issue exists to expose how attention from others stabilizes your behavior over time.

It teaches you to notice when:

  • you are performing for an audience
  • your responses are shaped by who is watching
  • agreement and conflict both reinforce your position
  • visibility increases pressure to stay consistent

…and how observation becomes a form of containment.

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

You react.
Someone sees it.
They respond.
They like it.
They expect more of it.

Now it’s not just a moment.
It’s a pattern.

The audience forms around that pattern.

  • people who agree stay
  • people who oppose return
  • both reinforce the role

Now you’re not just reacting…
you’re maintaining something.

A tone.
A position.
A version of yourself.

Even silence starts to feel noticeable.

You feel the pull:

“They’re expecting something from me.”

So you deliver.
Again.
And again.

Until the role isn’t just yours.
It’s shared.

🛑 INTERRUPT IT

You don’t need to disappear.
You need to stop performing.

Notice when your awareness shifts outward:

“How will this be received?”
“What will they think?”
“What do they expect me to say?”

That’s the moment.

Pause.

Yeah. Still you.
Still watching.
Good.

Ask:

  • Would I say this if no one saw it?
  • Am I expressing… or maintaining?
  • What happens if I don’t deliver anything here?

Feel the pressure drop.

You don’t owe the audience continuity.

Break it:

  • don’t respond
  • respond differently
  • don’t reinforce your usual tone
  • let the pattern weaken

You’re allowed to exit without explanation.

🏢 NON-CORPORATE NOTICE

This publication does not track engagement.

No metrics are collected.
No performance is measured.
No audience is required.

Any sense of being watched
was established elsewhere.

NOTICE • REFUSE • CHOOSE
🧠 You don’t owe them a reaction.
