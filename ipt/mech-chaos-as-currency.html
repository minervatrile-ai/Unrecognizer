💰 CHAOS AS CURRENCY

When Conflict Becomes the Product

What It Feels Like

Nothing ever resolves.

New controversies replace old ones instantly.

Calm feels naïve.

The churn never stops.

What’s Actually Happening

Chaos as currency treats unresolved conflict as a revenue stream.

Stability reduces engagement.

Division sustains attention.

Conflict becomes inventory.

Why It Works

Uncertainty keeps people checking back.

Tension sustains arousal.

Resolution ends interaction.

Drama extends shelf life.

How It’s Exploited

Influencers avoid closure.

Platforms surface disputes over solutions.

Peace is framed as complacency.

Stalemate is profitable.

The Hidden Cost

Cynicism grows.

Trust erodes.

People disengage from real-world problem solving.

Everything feels performative.

De-activation Cue

If nothing is allowed to end, pause.

Unending conflict serves someone.

Resolution is not weakness.
