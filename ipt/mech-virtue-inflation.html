🧠 VIRTUE INFLATION

When Disagreement Becomes Moral Heroism

What It Feels Like

Being right feels important.

Being opposed feels noble.

Disagreement feels like proof of character.

Moral intensity substitutes for understanding.

What’s Actually Happening

Virtue inflation amplifies moral stakes to stabilize identity.

Ordinary differences are reframed as ethical battles.

The self feels larger when positioned against wrongdoing.

Moral posture replaces inquiry.

Why It Works

Righteousness delivers instant self-worth.

Clarity feels better than complexity.

Moral certainty quiets doubt.

Feeling good is mistaken for being correct.

How It’s Exploited

Influencers frame issues as tests of virtue.

Nuance is portrayed as weakness or complicity.

Outrage becomes a badge of moral seriousness.

The hotter the take, the higher the status.

The Hidden Cost

Curiosity collapses.

Conversation becomes theater.

People compete for purity instead of insight.

Problems harden instead of resolving.

De-activation Cue

If moral intensity is rising faster than understanding, pause.

Virtue doesn’t need inflation.

Truth survives without performance.
