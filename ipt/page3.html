⚙️ CORE FUNCTION

At its most stripped-down level:

👉 Identity is the pattern by which a being experiences itself as itself

It answers:

  • Who am I?
  • What belongs to me?
  • Where do I fit?
  • What must I protect?
  • What would feel like betrayal?

Identity is not just labeling.

Without it:

  • 🧩 harder to interpret
  • 🛡 harder to defend
  • 📡 harder to project forward

🧱 STRUCTURE: IDENTITY IS MULTI-LAYERED

There is no single core.

There are layers:

  • 🧬 Bodily — this body is me
  • 📖 Autobiographical — this happened to me
  • 👥 Social — I am one of these people
  • ⚖️ Moral — I stand for this
  • 🎭 Role-based — I am a parent / worker / artist
  • 🔮 Aspirational — I am becoming this
  • 📚 Narrative — my life means this
  • 🛡 Defensive — I am not that

⚠️ Conflict arises when these layers disagree.
