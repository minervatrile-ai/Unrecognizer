🧠⚠️ IPT: TMT MANIPULATION CHECKLIST

Spotting existential leverage in real posts

📡 STEP 1: SCAN FOR MORTALITY SIGNALS

(Is fear of loss/death being quietly activated?)

Look for:

  • “Everything is collapsing”
  • “This is the end of…”
  • “We’re being replaced / erased”
  • constant crisis framing

🧩 IPT Translation:
👉 Mortality isn’t stated… it’s implied

⚠️ STEP 2: DETECT IDENTITY THREAT

(Is your sense of self being poked?)

Look for:

  • “People like you…”
  • “Your values are under attack”
  • “They hate what you stand for”

🧩 IPT Translation:
👉 Not information… activation

👑 STEP 3: SYMBOL LOADING

(Is a person becoming a survival symbol?)

Look for:

  • “He’s the only one telling the truth”
  • “If they fall, we fall”
  • extreme loyalty framing

🧩 IPT Translation:
👉 Human → Symbol → Shield

🔥 STEP 4: OUTRAGE IGNITION

(Did your emotional temperature spike fast?)

Look for:

  • instant anger
  • urgency to react/share
  • “this is insane” / “unacceptable”

🧩 IPT Translation:
👉 Velocity over clarity

🧨 STEP 5: US vs THEM SIMPLIFICATION

(Has the world been split clean in two?)

Look for:

  • “they’re the problem”
  • “these people are dangerous”
  • moral absolutism

🧩 IPT Translation:
👉 Complexity collapsed into enemy

🧱 STEP 6: DEHUMANIZATION SHORTCUT

(Are people reduced to labels?)

Look for:

  • stereotypes
  • dismissive categories
  • no nuance, no individuality

🧩 IPT Translation:
👉 Easier to attack a label than a person

🔁 STEP 7: LOOP REINFORCEMENT

(Is the post feeding itself?)

Look for:

  • repeated emotional hooks
  • algorithm-friendly outrage
  • comments escalating intensity

🧩 IPT Translation:
👉 Engagement = fuel

🧭 STEP 8: FALSE RESOLUTION

(Is a “solution” offered after activation?)

Look for:

  • “follow me”
  • “join this”
  • “this is the only way”

🧩 IPT Translation:
👉 Fear created → identity offered

⚙️ QUICK SCAN VERSION (IN THE WILD)

Run this like a mental checklist:

📡 Is fear being activated?

⚠️ Is identity being threatened?

👑 Is someone becoming a symbol?

🔥 Am I heating up fast?

🧨 Is this turning into “us vs them”?

🧱 Are people being simplified?

🔁 Is this looping emotionally?

🧭 Is a solution being pushed?

👉 If you hit 4+ of these…
you’re likely inside a TMT manipulation sequence

🧠 IPT COUNTER-MOVE

Don’t argue the content.

Interrupt the chain:

  • slow down your response
  • separate signal from story
  • restore human complexity
  • refuse forced urgency

🧩 FINAL FRAME

Most manipulation doesn’t say:

“Be afraid of death.”

It says:

“Something you are is under threat.”

And your system responds the same way.

⚙️ ONE-LINE TOOL

“Is this informing me… or stabilizing me through fear?”
