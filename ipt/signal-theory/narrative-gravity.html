🧠 THE MECHANISM: NARRATIVE GRAVITY 🧲

“Some stories don’t just spread… they start pulling.”

⚙️ WHY THIS FOLLOWS MIGRATION

Migration = movement
Gravity = accumulation + pull

Once a narrative has:

  • traveled
  • mutated
  • repeated

…it begins to gain mass.
And mass changes everything.

🧲 WHAT NARRATIVE GRAVITY IS

The point where a narrative becomes:

  • 👉 easier to fall into than question
  • 👉 more natural to repeat than examine
  • 👉 the “default interpretation” in a space

It stops moving like a rumor…
and starts sitting like truth-weight.

🧠 CORE EFFECT

Instead of people spreading the narrative…
👉 the narrative starts organizing people around it.

⚙️ HOW IT FORMS

1. 🔁 Repetition Density

You’ve seen it enough times that it feels familiar
→ familiarity becomes credibility

2. 🧩 Cross-Source Reinforcement

Different people say similar versions
→ appears independently verified

3. 🧠 Cognitive Ease

It becomes:

  • quick to understand
  • easy to recall
  • simple to repeat

→ low resistance = high adoption

4. 🏷 Social Anchoring

It attaches to:

  • groups
  • identities
  • values

→ now rejecting it has social cost

🧬 WHAT CHANGES WHEN GRAVITY KICKS IN

🌀 Interpretation Bends

New information gets pulled into the narrative:
“see? this proves it.”

🧱 Alternatives Collapse

Other explanations feel:

  • weaker
  • irrelevant
  • suspicious

🎭 Neutrality Disappears

You’re no longer just observing.
👉 you’re either:

  • aligned
  • or resisting

📡 DETECTION SIGNALS

You’re looking at Narrative Gravity when:

  • people assume it without stating it
  • conversations start from it, not toward it
  • disagreement feels like disruption, not discussion
  • phrases become shorthand (no explanation needed)

🟩 GREEN CHANNEL VIEW

Migration asks:
“where is this going?”

Gravity asks:
“what is this pulling into itself?”

⚠️ WHY THIS MATTERS

Because once gravity forms…

You’re no longer evaluating a narrative.
👉 You’re inside its field.

And inside the field:

  • evidence bends
  • language compresses
  • identity locks faster

🧠 IPT TRANSLATION

👉 Signal enters
👉 Narrative forms
👉 Narrative migrates
👉 Narrative gains mass
👉 Narrative begins to pull

🔚 FINAL LINE

It doesn’t need to convince you anymore.
It just needs you to stand nearby.
